## Essential Directives
1. **Authoritative Docs**: Read [docs/PROJECT.md](docs/PROJECT.md), [docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md](docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md) (v2.1), [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), [docs/BACKLOG.md](docs/BACKLOG.md), and [docs/HANDOFF.md](docs/HANDOFF.md) before writing code.

3. **Verification**: Verify every change with automated tests and documented evidence.
4. **Handoff Discipline**: Keep [docs/HANDOFF.md](docs/HANDOFF.md) updated with implemented work, verified results, and the next task.
